## Current Research Direction

I am studying how multimodal AI models distinguish direct user instructions from untrusted or conflicting information displayed within webpages, documents, notifications, and desktop applications.

The research also examines whether safeguards such as explicit consent, source labeling, contextual boundaries, least-privilege access, and confirmation requirements can reduce prompt injection, unsafe compliance, hallucinations, and unintended actions.
